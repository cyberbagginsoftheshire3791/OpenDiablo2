# Bug database and launch ledger

The error log Constitution I.3 and Plan §7 require, and the home Bethke's *"bugs
without owners simply will not be addressed"* asks for. Every known defect gets a
row: **id · first seen · build · reproduction · rate · owner · status.** A bug
without an owner is not logged. New defects are added here the day they are found,
not at some future audit.

Seeded 14 September 2026 by the hardening burst with the five defects that were
living as prose under "Parked" in `state.md`. The three map-load faces are parked
together on Josh's ruling (28 Aug): `MapEngine.asset` is a concrete type, so there
is no seam to inject a failing loader, and a nil guard would make the game *quiet
rather than correct* — **do not "just add the return"**; they go into the DT1
milestone with the loader-interface extraction that makes them testable. All are
intermittent: **re-run before investigating.**

Added 17 September 2026 (Cowork): **BUG-8 and BUG-9**, the two sight defects. An
independent Codex audit of `72bc622c` found the `checkLos` endpoint overshoot; a
verification pass reproduced it with a line-for-line port and measured its incidence
over the signed notice radius. BUG-9 was visible in the d2-formats reference as an
as-found observation and had never been given a row. **Both were given owners by Josh the same day:**
BUG-8 to **the sight burst**, a burst of its own ruled to run BEFORE M4.4c-2a --
because c-2a exists to measure pacing, and an awareness ray that is wrong on 57%
of casts would have had the first real numbers measured through a broken sensor.
BUG-9 to the DT1 milestone. **Neither is fixed by this commit** -- neither is in
c-2's signed scope, which is why BUG-8 got a burst rather than a place in it.

Added 19 September 2026 (Cowork), after Josh launched the game for the first time
and could do nothing but walk around and die: **BUG-10 and BUG-11**, and BUG-9
shipped. The three are one story told from three sides -- the night went quiet
because sight refused rays at eight tiles (BUG-9), because a beaten pack held a
slot in the group cap until sunrise (BUG-11), and because a routed beast is
unwatched forever and stands where it broke (BUG-10, the half still open). **All
three were invisible to every instrument this project owns**, and the reason is
the same one the 19 September audit is about: every test tested a part. See
`The Sight Burst - S0 Measurements.md` for the evidence and
`playtest/unaided_test.go` for the gate that was missing.

Added later the same evening: **BUG-12**, and it retracts BUG-10's headline. The
census BUG-10 itself asked for was finally runnable once sight and the cap were
fixed, and it found two things. The eighteen "live monsters standing inside the
notice radius" were **Akara, Charsi, Gheed, Kashya, Warriv, five rogues, five
chickens and three cows** — D2's own Act 1 Town population, which the tables
never placed and the notice model therefore never watched. And **rout had never
fired in any unaided run**, because a pack's members were being placed on
opposite sides of the player, so only ever one of them was in a fight. That is
BUG-12, it is why the record's "zero routs across six nights" and
"quick-resolve never firing in 662 readings" looked like sight or pacing
problems, and fixing it produced **the first measured routs in this project's
history**. It also voided the evening's second dial sweep, which is the third
time in one day a dial was tuned over a mechanism that did not work yet.
